Returns and Refunds

Our Commitment to Your Satisfaction

As a professional agency, our priority is to deliver high-quality services that meet your expectations. We understand that sometimes issues may arise, and we are committed to resolving them promptly and fairly.

 

Digital Deliverables and Services

Since our services involve digital deliverables, there are no physical products to return. Instead, we focus on ensuring the quality and accuracy of the services provided.

 

Refund Policy

  • Initial Consultations: Fees for initial consultations are generally non-refundable. However, if you are not satisfied with the consultation, please reach out to us, and we will do our best to address your concerns.
  • Service Fees: For ongoing projects and services, refunds will be considered on a case-by-case basis. If you are not satisfied with the work delivered, please contact us within 2 days of receiving the deliverable to discuss your concerns.
  • Revision Policy: We offer 3 rounds of revisions to ensure your satisfaction with our services. If, after these revisions, you are still not satisfied, we will consider a partial or full refund based on the specific circumstances and the amount of work completed.
  • Prepaid Packages: For prepaid service packages, refunds will be prorated based on the work completed up to the point of cancellation.

 

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please follow these steps:

  1. Contact Us: Email us at hh@rabbitmarketinghouse.in
  2. Provide Details: Include your project details, the specific concerns you have, and any supporting information that can help us understand the issue.
  3. Resolution Process: Our team will review your request and get back to you within 2 business days to discuss a resolution. This may include additional revisions, a partial refund, or a full refund depending on the circumstances.

 

Exceptions and Special Cases

  • Force Majeure: Refunds may not be issued for delays or issues caused by events beyond our control, such as natural disasters, technical failures, or other unforeseen circumstances.
  • Client Responsibilities: Refunds may not be granted if the dissatisfaction is due to the client’s failure to provide necessary information, feedback, or materials in a timely manner.

 

Dispute Resolution

If a resolution cannot be reached through our standard refund process, we are committed to finding an amicable solution through mediation or other dispute resolution methods.

 

Contact Information

For any questions or concerns regarding our returns and refunds policy, please do not hesitate to contact us:

  • Email: hh@rabbitmarketinghouse.in

We value your business and aim to ensure that you are completely satisfied with our services. Thank you for your understanding and cooperation.
